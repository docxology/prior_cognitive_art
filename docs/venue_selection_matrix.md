# Venue Selection Matrix

Date: 2026-07-07

This matrix is a decision aid for selecting a target venue or preprint route.
It deliberately avoids recommending a specific outlet because venue scope,
fees, formatting rules, and editorial policy can change. Check the current
venue pages before making a submission decision.

## Scoring

Score each candidate from 1 to 5 for each criterion.

| Criterion | Favorable signal | Risk signal |
|---|---|---|
| Scope fit | Publishes conceptual work on cognitive science, active inference, philosophy of science, theoretical biology, or Bayesian methodology. | Requires empirical datasets, simulations, or formal proofs beyond the current manuscript scope. |
| Article type | Allows theory, perspective, target article, essay, or working-paper style submissions. | Only accepts empirical research reports or invited-only article types. |
| Interdisciplinary tolerance | Welcomes cross-field synthesis and careful boundary-setting. | Strongly discipline-specific review norms make the paper appear out of category. |
| Markov blanket debate fit | Reviewers are likely to understand both active-inference use and critique-sensitive boundary claims. | Reviewers may expect an exhaustive Markov blanket controversy review. |
| Figure tolerance | Allows conceptual diagrams and visual argument maps. | Treats figures mainly as empirical results or requires data-backed plots. |
| Length and reference budget | Word count and citation policy can accommodate the argument and 40-source bibliography. | Strict limits would force removal of necessary scope-control passages. |
| Open access and cost | Fees and licensing are acceptable for the author. | Fees, licensing, or copyright terms conflict with the intended release. |
| Review speed and audience | Timeline and readership match the author's publication goals. | Long review queue or narrow readership reduces the value of submission. |
| Preprint compatibility | Permits prior preprint circulation if a preprint route is chosen. | Policies restrict preprint posting or public working-paper circulation. |

## Decision Rule

1. Exclude venues that require empirical or simulation results.
2. Exclude venues whose current policy conflicts with the intended preprint or
   repository release.
3. Prefer candidates with strong scope fit, article-type fit, and
   interdisciplinary tolerance.
4. Use `docs/reviewer_risk_register.md` to test whether likely reviewer
   objections are expected and answerable for the venue's audience.
5. Only after a target is selected, adapt formatting, cover letter, declarations,
   and reference style.

## Preprint Route

A preprint route is appropriate if the immediate goal is circulation,
timestamping, and feedback before journal targeting. Before posting, confirm:

- author metadata and affiliations
- license choice
- repository or archival bundle
- no confidential material
- final PDF and figure rendering
- source and citation closure
- journal policy compatibility if a later journal submission is intended
