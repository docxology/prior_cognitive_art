# Scholarship Audit

Date: 2026-07-10

This audit records the publication-readiness scholarship decisions for a
conceptual manuscript with one deterministic illustrative simulation trace.
Sources were promoted only when they directly changed claim boundaries, formal
inventory, prose, or figure contracts.

The 2026-07-10 continuation pass did not pad the bibliography. It attacked
the publication oracle and dashboard provenance surfaces, keeping the existing
source set while tightening the distinction between scholarly support,
source-owned claims, and generated presentation artifacts.
A high-depth Perplexity synthesis query timed out during the first readiness
pass; that work was split into targeted Markov blanket, niche construction,
hyperprior, active-inference, and Tinbergen/Konner checks and then verified
against direct bibliographic records where available. A later 2026-07-08
`sonar-deep-research` pass focused on the title's art claim, material
engagement, imagination, and cultural niche construction; only stable scholarly
sources that changed live claims or figures were promoted. A caption-focused
follow-up pass used Perplexity as discovery, then verified accepted sources
against direct publisher, PubMed/Europe PMC, JSTOR, Springer, Sage, Cambridge,
or ScienceDirect records before editing captions, prose, and the claim ledger.
The 2026-07-09 upstream-bias continuation added first-prior, co-homeostasis,
allostasis, developmental visceral-dynamics, morphogenesis, ecological active
inference, and evolutionary active-inference sources because they tightened the
"before the first prior" claim boundary.

## Rejected or deferred sources

| Source family | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Social/normative-prior expansion literature | Deferred. It would broaden the paper toward social epistemology rather than sharpening the current prior-termination and aesthetic-boundary argument. |
| Additional Markov blanket critique responses beyond Bruineberg/Raja | Deferred. The accepted pair is enough to bound the claim without turning the paper into a literature review of the controversy. |
| Empirical active-inference applications | Rejected for this pass. The manuscript contract is conceptual and permits only a deterministic illustrative trace, so empirical performance examples would create an unsupported evidential layer. |
| New stochastic or numerical attractor/landscape sources | Rejected for this pass. Fixed-point and attractor language remains deterministic and conceptual; no quantitative dynamical claim is introduced. |
| Treating developmental niche construction as a fourth termination type | Rejected. The accepted literature supports a constraint on termination, not a new stop in the formal inventory. |
| Treating predictive processing as a complete theory of art | Rejected. The accepted aesthetics sources support expectation/ambiguity/meaning framing, while 4E and neuroaesthetic critique block reduction to prediction-error reward. |
| Blog, video, store, encyclopedia, and lab-profile results from the 2026-07-08 Perplexity run | Rejected for manuscript integration. They were useful only as discovery hints; none was promoted without a stable scholarly book, journal, or publisher record. |
| Treating material engagement as proof that artifacts literally contain predictive models | Rejected. Material-engagement scholarship supports artifact-mediated cognition and creative discovery, not the stronger claim that an artwork itself is a neural or generative model. |
| Hohwy video/seminar and non-scholarly explainer leads surfaced during caption research | Rejected for citation integration. They were useful discovery cues, but the caption claims were instead backed by peer-reviewed papers, monographs, and publisher records. |
| Additional active-inference or Markov-blanket review papers beyond the accepted 2012/2023 additions | Deferred. The captions needed one action-loop anchor and one recent FEP scope anchor, not a broad expansion of the active-inference literature review. |

## Manuscript Changes Driven by the Audit

- Markov blanket prose now distinguishes model-level conditional-independence
  structure, realized organism-environment coupling, and critique boundaries.
- Niche construction prose now names the developmental/selective distinction and
  extended-active-inference role without promoting a fourth termination mode.
- Hyperprior prose now treats stopping as pragmatic modeling discipline rather
  than a proof that regress has disappeared.
- Tinbergen/Konner prose now presents Konner as a cautious extension of
  explanatory-level pluralism.
- The caption pass added Bergman and Beehner's causes/consequences leveling as
  a pressure test on the crossed-axis figures without replacing the manuscript's
  visual grammar.
- Active-inference and variational-bound captions now cite direct agency and
  2023 Physics Reports sources rather than relying only on broader FEP prose.
- Niche-construction captions and conclusion now distinguish foundational
  ecological/cultural niche construction from the variational niche-construction
  extension already used in the paper.
- Variational-free-energy prose, formal claim F10, and the corresponding figure
  caption now avoid the old literal zero-free-energy/settling-point overclaim.
- The introduction, methodology, conclusion, related work, and claim ledger now
  define "art" through predictive aesthetics, music enculturation,
  neuroaesthetic triads, 4E affordance accounts, and anti-reduction cautions.
- Five deterministic figures were added to make these boundaries visible:
  `source_claim_boundary_map`, `markov_blanket_scope_split`, and
  `niche_prior_feedback_field`, plus the new
  `aesthetic_prior_craft_map` and `first_prior_grounding_stack`.
- The 2026-07-08 pass added two claim-ledger rows for material prior-craft and
  cultural prior-craft boundaries, and expanded the existing aesthetic boundary
  row with imagination and improvisation cautions.
- The 2026-07-09 upstream pass added the `first-prior-grounding-stack`
  claim-ledger row and integrated the requested PMID `33872985` source into the
  introduction, methodology, visual atlas, and related-work scope boundary.
