## Scope and thesis

This remains a conceptual paper. Its precise thesis is that the explanatory
kind must be identified before asking why a prior exists, and that a proposed
answer must name its termination rule. Pragmatic, selection, and fixed-point
termination are the paper's organizing families, not an exhaustive taxonomy.
The project retains one deterministic illustrative formal-state trace and no
stochastic simulation, synthetic data, empirical effect estimate, or numerical
performance claim.
