## Letter

Dear `[Editor name]`,

Please consider our manuscript, "Prior Cognitive Art: Tinbergen Axes,
Hyperprior Regress, and Fixed-Point Explanation," for `[Submission type]` in
`[Venue name]`.

The manuscript asks why a prior exists without treating the answer as a simple
search for an earlier prior. It separates mechanism, function, ontogeny, and
phylogeny as distinct explanatory questions; compares ontogenetic regress with
the Bayesian hyperprior problem; and argues that the regress stops only when a
practice introduces a termination rule: pragmatic weak-prior closure, selection
filtering, or self-referential fixed-point organization.

The contribution is conceptual and methodological. It does not report empirical
results or simulations. Instead, it provides a bounded argument inventory, a
claim ledger, and 19 deterministic conceptual figures that make the explanatory
boundaries inspectable. The manuscript also includes a deliberately bounded
Markov blanket treatment, separating model-level use from realized-boundary
interpretations and critique-sensitive metaphysical overreach.

We believe the paper will be relevant to readers interested in active
inference, philosophy of cognitive science, Bayesian modeling, biological
organization, and explanatory methodology. Its central value is not a new
empirical result but a disciplined map of how different explanatory practices
stop prior regress without collapsing into one another.

The project contains no human-subject, animal-subject, clinical, or field data.
The code associated with the manuscript is used to render deterministic
conceptual figures, maintain source/manuscript consistency, and validate the
claim inventory. `[Declarations required by venue]`

Thank you for considering the manuscript.

Sincerely,
